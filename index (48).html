01

Building the foundation, and watching where it leads.

I'm in my third year of Computer Science at York, and the part of tech I find most interesting is what happens once the code works: whether a tool actually changes how people get their jobs done.

That's a big reason my internship at Visionet stuck with me. I spent the summer inside the proposal process, reading RFPs and drafting responses, and ended up building an AI tool the business development team now uses in production.

I keep up with AI and emerging tech well past what's on the syllabus, partly because it moves so fast that last month's answer is often already out of date. I'm early in my career and still building my foundation, and honestly, that's the fun part.

Outside of all that, it's cars. Formula 1 most of all.
